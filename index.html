The company

Building intelligence for the physical world.

The physical infrastructure that keeps cities running is complex. Roads, drainage systems, pumps, gates, waterways, buildings, hospitals, power systems, and transportation networks all interact. Diluvium is developing software that connects environmental conditions with infrastructure decisions.

◈

Understand

↗

Act

Evaluate possible actions and recommend strategies that can reduce damage and disruption.

The problem

Extreme events are becoming infrastructure decision problems.

During a major event, infrastructure operators may have large amounts of data but limited time to determine what to do with it.

Disconnected information

Forecasts, sensors, maps, infrastructure records, and operational information can live in separate systems.

Rapidly changing conditions

Rainfall, river levels, drainage capacity, and infrastructure conditions can change throughout an event.

Interconnected infrastructure

A decision involving one pump, gate, or drainage area can affect conditions somewhere else.

High consequences

Poor decisions can increase flood depth, extend disruption, damage assets, and affect critical services.

How it works

From raw data to operational decisions.

The goal is not simply to display another map or forecast. Diluvium is being designed to answer the operational question: “What should we do?”

01 / INPUT

Data

Weather forecasts, rainfall, water levels, terrain, maps, infrastructure information, sensors, and other relevant data.

02 / MODEL

Simulation

Model how the event could affect the physical system under different conditions.

03 / DECIDE

Optimization

Compare possible infrastructure actions and identify strategies that can improve outcomes.
